Fishing

Stone Mountain Lake

Hours & Access
Fishing is permitted in Stone Mountain Lake from 6:00 AM until midnight. Access to the park is included with a daily or annual parking pass. A valid Georgia Fishing License is required.
Fish in the Lake
🐟 Bass
🐠 Carp
🐡 Crappie
🐟 Catfish
🐡 Bream
Boating Rules
• Private boats allowed daily during daylight hours
• Launch from the Public Boat Ramp near Evergreen Pavilion
• Outboard motors 10 hp max
• No alcoholic beverages on privately-owned boats

Golf Club

Two Championship 18-Hole Courses

Overview
Located minutes from downtown Atlanta, Stone Mountain Golf Club features two championship courses with scenic views of historic Stone Mountain and Stone Mountain Lake.
Stonemont Course
Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. in 1969. A par 70 layout stretching 6,863 yards with narrow fairways, strategically placed bunkers and numerous stands of Georgia pines.

Has hosted numerous national professional events and Georgia amateur championships.
Par 70 6,863 Yards Bent Grass Greens
Lakemont Course
Designed by Georgia native John LaFoy. The front 9 wind around Stone Mountain Lake with 6 holes having water in play. The back 9 features rock outcroppings with 4 water holes and dramatic elevation changes.
Lake Views 10 Water Holes Elevation Changes

Rules & Regulations

Park policies for a safe and enjoyable visit

⚠️ River Notice
We are located on the Etowah River. During periods of heavy rain, mud and standing water may occur. Please remember that camping is an outdoor activity.
Check-In & Check-Out
Check-In Time
2:00 PM. Early check-in fees may apply. Call for early arrival availability.
Check-Out Time
11:00 AM. Late check-outs after 11 AM will incur a late check-out fee — call for availability. All guests must depart by 1:00 PM or an additional night will be charged.
Cancellation Policy
Cancellations made 7 days or more from the date of arrival will be refunded the deposit amount minus a $20 cancellation fee. Cancellations made within 7 days of arrival are non-refundable.
Refund Policy
No refunds are given for early departures or for early departure due to guest behavior or ejection.
RVs & Vehicles
RV Rental Companies
Please obtain approval prior to bringing in a rental RV. Rental companies must be approved by management.
RV Types Allowed
All RVs must be self-contained and RVIA certified per city ordinance. No tents, truck tents, bus conversions, enclosed trailers or homemade RVs of any kind unless RVIA/ANSI certified.
Vehicle Washing
Washing RVs or vehicles is not permitted due to our proximity to the Etowah River.
Parking
Observe all parking signs and do not park on grass or empty sites. Parking passes must be displayed at all times while on park property. Failure to do so may result in being towed at owner's expense. Improperly parked, broken down, or inoperable vehicles may also be towed.
Golf Carts
Personal carts are allowed but must be declared at the time of booking. Proof of insurance must be provided. A registration fee applies for bringing a personal golf cart. The 10 MPH speed limit must be obeyed at all times, and only licensed drivers may operate golf carts on park property.
EVs
EV charging is not permitted onsite.
Quiet Hours & Conduct
🌙 Quiet Time
10 PM – 7 AM Sun–Thur  |  11 PM – 7 AM Fri & Sat
Speed Limit
10 MPH throughout the entire park.
Video Recording / Drones
For the safety and privacy of all guests, unauthorized video recording is strictly prohibited. Drones are not permitted at any time. Prior approval is required from management if you wish to film on property for any social media, monetized, or commercial use.
Not Permitted
No loud profanity, illegal drug use, public drunkenness, no open carry of firearms, no loud music, no fireworks, no disorderly conduct, no 4-wheelers, unauthorized video recording for commercial use, and no drones.
Conduct
Winding Waters is a family friendly park, and we want everyone to enjoy their stay. No obnoxious, offensive language, inappropriate behavior or disruptive activities are allowed. You will be ejected if you fail to comply — management reserves the right to determine these actions and act accordingly. No refund will be provided for early departures. If ejected, no refunds will be given.
Visitors & Gate Codes
Visitors
Registered guests may have a limited number of visitors at their site only, based on maximum site occupancy. Visitors must stop and check in at the office and obtain a visitor's site pass. If a visitor wants to use any of the amenities, a visitor's amenity pass must be purchased at time of entry — there is a fee per visitor and it's only available if park occupancy limits allow. The resort reserves the right to refuse any day visitor based on resort capacity. Guests are responsible for their visitors' actions and all visitors must leave by quiet hours. We reserve the right to deny visitors and amenity access based on park capacity. Any visitor vehicle must fit at the guest's RV site and may not park in any grass, roadway, or empty site.
Gate Codes
Gate codes are for registered guests only and may not be given out or shared with anyone, including visitors or delivery drivers.
Mail / Packages / Deliveries
No mail or packages may be delivered to the park — there is a post office and Amazon lockers very close by. Mail and packages sent to the park will be returned. No food or grocery deliveries to your site; you must meet food deliveries at the front of the park.
Site & Waste
Sites
All sites must be kept clean, free of clutter, pet debris, cigarette butts and other trash. Do not interfere with landscaping or lawn maintenance.
🗑️ Garbage
Please put all trash in the appropriate receptacle — placing trash outside or near the receptacle may result in fines. Household bagged trash only. No dumping of appliances, mattresses, tires, hazardous materials (including motor oil), or other large items. Large dumpsters are marked on the map; do not leave trash bags outside due to wildlife.
Fire Rings & Grills
Do not move fire rings or grills. No burning of trash, pet bags, cigarette butts, or any other items other than firewood. No unattended fires. Guest is responsible for any damages to park property caused by placing devices on park property. Do not gather firewood from park landscaping or disrupt natural vegetation.
Firewood
In compliance with the USDA & Georgia Forestry Commission, bringing firewood into the park from out of state is prohibited due to the devastation of transporting destructive foreign pests. No firewood from outside of Georgia is permitted.
Sewer Hoses
All sewer hoses must have a sewer ring or elbow and be supported off the ground per city ordinance.
Pool & Hot Tub
No lifeguard on duty, swim at your own risk. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult at all times and children under 5 are not allowed in the hot tub. Swim diapers are required for Georgia law compliance, and for anyone not toilet trained. No glass, pets, smoking, vaping, or tobacco products of any kind are allowed in the pool area. Pool passes must be worn at all times in the pool area. No food is permitted inside the pool — food is allowed on the pool deck away from the water. The water slides have a strict height requirement of 48″ per the manufacturer, no exceptions.
🐾 Pets
Pets must be leashed at all times when outside your RV per city ordinance. Pets may never be left unattended outside. No runs or staking is allowed at your site. Pets are not allowed in the pool area or restrooms. Aggressive behavior from any pet will result in that pet being asked to leave the park. You must pick up after your pet — failure to do so can result in fines or expulsion from the park. Proof of current vaccinations must be presented upon request. Use of resort dog parks are at your own risk.
Liability & Compliance
By making a reservation and staying on property you agree to abide and follow the above rules and policies. Please observe and obey all signs and postings displayed throughout the park and be considerate of other campers. Violation of these rules and policies could result in loss of privileges, fines and up to and including ejection from the park. Winding Waters RV Resort reserves the right to alter, change or modify any of these rules and policies at any time whether written or verbal. Management's decision in all disputes is deemed final.
Release & Indemnification
I agree to release, indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Winding Waters RV Resort, its owners, affiliates and employees from and against any and all claims, demands, causes of action, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, or expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or related to: (a) injury, death, or property damage caused by falling trees, limbs, branches, or other natural conditions; (b) inherent risks associated with camping, RVing, outdoor recreation, or use of the premises; or (c) Participant's/Guest's presence on or use of the property, except to the extent caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Winding Waters RV Resort.

Park Information
🌙 Quiet Hours
11 PM to 7 AM. For noise complaints call the SMP Police Non-Emergency Number 770-498-5675.
🗑️ Garbage Pickup
Curbside at your site between 9 AM – 4 PM. There are no dumpsters — trash will be picked up curbside only.
👕 Laundry Facilities
Located at Comfort Stations #3, #7 and #9. Use the Aaxon Pay App. A QR Code is in the Laundry Room.
🔍 Lost & Found
Located at the Welcome Center. Technology items (phones, computers, wallets, credit cards) will be turned over to SMP Public Safety.
📦 Mail Drop & Package Delivery
At the Welcome Center. Please include your Name and Site number when having something delivered.
🏊 Swimming Pool Passes
Available in the Welcome Center. You must present one for yourself or family when asked by a staff member.
🚗 UBER / LYFT
Available for guests. Drivers will be charged a $5.00 fee at the entrance gate — please advise your driver in advance.
⚡ Weather Alert System
If lightning is detected within 10 miles of the park a horn will sound once for 15 seconds. The horn sounds 3 times rapidly when lightning has cleared the area. If severe weather is approaching, the system will sound advising you to seek shelter.
Emergency Shelters
